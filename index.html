Point of view — 2026

Should we pay
for AI skills?

My working view: not for the label. Pay should move when AI changes the market value of the work — its scarcity, its scope, its accountabilities. The premium is never the first question. What changed is.

AI changes the tooling AI changes the work
  1. 01 Fluency Copilots, prompting, AI in everyday work. Not a premium. A rising baseline for the role — skill expectations and performance, not pay.
  2. 02 Scarce capability Depth the market genuinely prices — production deployment, agent architecture, evaluation. Pay the person, not the job. Positioning, targeted retention, equity — governed, and dated, because scarcity decays.
  3. 03 Role transformation Scope and accountabilities shift until the benchmark stops describing the job. Reprice the job. Re-evaluate, re-level, rematch the market. A premium on a stale benchmark compounds the error.
  4. 04 New work A capability the architecture has no home for. Create the profile — last. Only when work content and organizational purpose justify it. Most new titles are old archetypes in new skill bundles.

Postings naming AI skills advertise premiums of roughly thirty to sixty percent. Control for level, firm and skill bundle and the defensible number is single-digit to low-teens — about what employers who differentiate actually pay. The spread is composition, not premium. .

The test case

Forward Deployed
Engineer.

One of the fastest-growing titles in enterprise go-to-market is also the least benchmarkable. Posted base ranges for the same four words run from roughly $105k to $385k, because the title covers at least four different jobs.

The work inside the roleWhere the market prices it
Ships production code in the customer’s stackSoftware engineering
Scopes the technical win with executivesSolutions engineering
Owns delivery on the customer’s siteProfessional services & consulting
Carries adoption long after go-liveCustomer success & technical account
Feeds the field back into the productProduct management

Benchmark the work, not the title. The blend — with level, labor market and incentive treatment — decides which references matter and how they triangulate. Posted ranges are market signals, not survey data; position against them follows compensation philosophy.

The operating model

Sense → Diagnose → Benchmark → Decide → Govern

Watch the signals before the exception requests arrive. Establish what changed — the work, or the tooling. Decompose the role and triangulate. Choose the mechanism. Then set the evidence bar, the review cadence and the sunset.

The discipline underneath it: keep job value, person value and temporary scarcity separate. Jobs get re-architected. People get positioned. Scarcity gets a mechanism with a review date — because base salary is permanent, and scarcity rarely is.
